# SAPH manuscript and supplementary material: sources and build

The sources of the manuscript and the supplementary material (Journal of Molecular Graphics and Modelling, JMGM, Elsevier;
research paper) and the tools that build them. The built documents are in 01_Manuscript and 02_Supplementary_Material of this
package; the facts behind every number are in 05_Tables (`facts.json`, `facts.md`), the tables in 05_Tables, the
figures in 04_Figures and the literature pool in 09_References.

- Main text: 8148 words of running prose, 6 keywords, 10 figures, 7 tables,
  1 equation, 93 references; Word file and a 44-page PDF preview.
- Supplementary material: a contents list, 16 sections, 10 figures, 40 tables, 134
  references; Word file and a 96-page PDF, and the ZIP file of machine-readable files (`SAPH_Supplementary_structures.zip`).
- Facts: 11111, of which 3556 were re-derived by `main/data/derive/d01`–`d17` (0
  disagreements).
- The checks that the documents passed at the final build (12 Oct 2026, 21:35–22:04 IST) are listed in VERIFICATION.md at
  the top of this package.

## Layout

| Path | What |
|---|---|
| `main/manuscript.md` | The paper (pandoc Markdown), building to `SAPH_manuscript.docx` and `.pdf`. Its YAML header holds the title, the author block (authors, affiliations, corresponding author, e-mail addresses, ORCID iDs), the abstract and the keywords. |
| `main/cover_letter.md` | The cover letter, building to `SAPH_cover_letter.docx`. |
| `main/highlights.md` | The 5 highlights, building to `SAPH_highlights.docx`. |
| `main/declaration_of_interest.md` | The declaration of interests in the wording of Elsevier's form, building to `SAPH_declaration_of_interest.docx`. |
| `main/references.json`, `si/references.json` | The Crossref records of the cited references (written by `lit.py bib`). |
| `main/data/build_facts.py` (+ `facts_*.py`) | Builds `facts.json` and `facts.md` (05_Tables), the only source of numbers for both documents: every fact with its source file and key, precision, caveats and withdrawn or superseded values. |
| `main/data/derive/d*_*.py` (`d01`–`d17`) | Independent re-derivations from the raw results (outputs in 05_Tables/facts_rederivations). `d14_completion.py` reads the PLBD rows of the hCA II controls (10_Reproducibility/inputs/plbd_hca2_azm_sfm.json); `d17_md.py` re-derives the facts of the molecular dynamics and of the two docking tests on hCA II from the series and the raw docking output (11_Molecular_dynamics/md, 07_Docking). |
| `main/data/build_tables.py` | Writes every table of the main text and of the supplementary material (05_Tables). A table title is one phrase or sentence; the rest of the caption prints as the first note under the table. |
| `main/figures/` | One script per figure in `scripts/`, the shared helpers, `captions.md` (the master captions; group A is generated by `scripts/captions_groupA.py`), `figure_params*.json`. `scripts/toc_graphic.py` draws the graphical abstract (03_Graphical_Abstract). |
| `si/manuscript.md` | The supplementary material: numbers prefixed S; tables and `sections/admet.md` pulled in by include lines; a contents list written by `crossref.lua`. It builds to `SAPH_Supplementary_Material.docx` and `.pdf`. |
| `si/figures/scripts/`, `si/compute/` | The figure scripts of the supplementary material, and the Fukui and ADMET computations. |
| `_shared/STYLE.md` | Writing rules: voice, banned phrases, notation, citations, docking wording, the structure of the paper. |
| `_shared/tools/` | `build.py` (journal profiles `--journal jics`, the default, `elsevier` and `acs-omega`), `lit.py`, `numbers_audit.py`, `overlap.py`, `lint_prose.py`, `docx_check.py`, the pandoc filters (`crossref.lua`, `include.lua`, `bibfix.lua`) and `manuscript.latex`. |
| `_shared/figkit/`, `_shared/csl/`, `_shared/reference.docx` | Figure toolkit; the citation styles (`elsevier-with-titles.csl`, used by the profiles `jics` and `elsevier`; `american-chemical-society.csl`, used by `acs-omega`) with the journal abbreviations (`journal_abbreviations.json`); the Word template. |
| `../ops/make_submission.py` | Builds the Word files of the cover letter, the highlights and the declaration of interests (`--letters`), and assembles the files to upload in `submission/JMGM/` of the repository. |

The source paths inside the scripts are those of the repository: `paper/main/data/facts.json` is 05_Tables/facts.json
here, `paper/main/tables` and `paper/si/tables` are 05_Tables, `paper/main/figures` and `paper/si/figures` are
04_Figures, `molecules/sfa/results` is 06_DFT_results, `dock/` is 07_Docking and `lit/` is 09_References
(10_Reproducibility/source_map.csv maps every file), and `md/` is 11_Molecular_dynamics/md.

## Toolchain

`build.py` expects at the repository root `tools/bin/pandoc`, `tools/paper-env` (Python 3.12 with lxml, python-docx,
matplotlib, pyvista, rdkit), `tools/texlive-extracted` (luaotfload) and `tools/fonts/extracted`; on the Raspberry Pi
these are symlinks made by `paper/_shared/tools/link_toolchain.sh`.

## How to rebuild (from a checkout of the repository)

```
P=/root/workspace/cqc-dft/tools/paper-env/bin/python

# 1. numbers: re-derivations, facts, tables (only if molecules/sfa/results, dock/ or md/ changed)
(cd paper/main/data/derive && for s in d01_xray d03_geometry d04_scan d05_freq d06_electronic d07_hirshfeld \
   d08_periodic d09_dock d10_rep_contacts d11_burst2 d12_revision d13_referee_calcs \
   d14_completion d15_control d16_jics d17_md; do nice $P $s.py; done)
nice $P paper/main/data/build_facts.py && nice $P paper/main/data/build_tables.py

# 2. figures and group-A captions (only if a figure changed)
nice -n 10 env OMP_NUM_THREADS=2 $P paper/main/figures/scripts/fig02_proton.py      # etc., one script per figure
nice -n 10 env OMP_NUM_THREADS=2 $P paper/main/figures/scripts/toc_graphic.py       # the graphical abstract (.tif, .png)
nice $P paper/main/figures/scripts/captions_groupA.py

# 3. checks and build (journal profile jics, the default)
python3 paper/_shared/tools/lint_prose.py main && python3 paper/_shared/tools/lint_prose.py si
python3 paper/_shared/tools/lint_prose.py paper/main/cover_letter.md paper/main/highlights.md
python3 paper/_shared/tools/numbers_audit.py main && python3 paper/_shared/tools/numbers_audit.py si
python3 paper/_shared/tools/numbers_audit.py main --manuscript paper/main/cover_letter.md
python3 paper/_shared/tools/numbers_audit.py main --manuscript paper/main/highlights.md
python3 paper/_shared/tools/overlap.py
nice tools/paper-env/bin/python paper/_shared/tools/build.py main    # Word + PDF; --spacing 2 = double spaced
nice tools/paper-env/bin/python paper/_shared/tools/build.py si
tools/paper-env/bin/python paper/_shared/tools/docx_check.py paper/main/SAPH_manuscript.docx \
    --crossref paper/main/build/crossref.json --roundtrip --strict
tools/paper-env/bin/python paper/_shared/tools/docx_check.py paper/si/SAPH_Supplementary_Material.docx \
    --crossref paper/si/build/crossref.json --roundtrip --strict

# 4. the cover letter, the highlights and the declaration of interests (Word files without line numbers)
python3 ops/make_submission.py --letters

# 5. the deliverables package and the submission folder. The package runs the checks again and writes the ZIP file of
#    the supplementary material; make_submission.py takes that file from the staging tree and writes submission/JMGM/.
nice python3 ops/package_deliverables.py --no-zip
python3 ops/make_submission.py
# (commit the repository here: the package records the id of the last commit)
nice python3 ops/package_deliverables.py
```

`python3 paper/_shared/tools/lit.py search|add|claim|verify|stats|table` manages the literature pool. Figure captions
live in `main/figures/captions.md` and, copied, in `main/manuscript.md`; the two are kept in step.

## Conventions of the text

- **Numbers.** Every printed number comes from `facts.json`; `numbers_audit.py` checks it. Rounding: half to even on
  the full-precision value.
- **References.** They enter only through `lit.py`: Crossref metadata and a retraction, withdrawal and
  expression-of-concern check; at most three keys per citation.
- **Journal style (JMGM, Elsevier).** Citation numbers in square brackets in order of first citation and the
  reference list in the Elsevier numbered style, with article titles and abbreviated journal names; "Fig. 1" and
  "Eq. (1)"; table titles of one phrase or sentence with the details in notes; every abbreviation defined at its
  first use in the text.
- **Level of theory.** B3LYP-D3(BJ)/6-311++G(d,p) is the primary level; TD-DFT uses CAM-B3LYP in IEF-PCM (water);
  model names are those of Table 1 (M1–M9). Periodic energies are PBE-D3(BJ) energy differences with a PBE0-D3(BJ)
  correction from single points; the periodic cost of the transfer is the constrained N1–H1A profile.
- **Docking.** Neutral sulfanilamide is the primary ligand, the sulfonamidate and the cation are sensitivity cases, and
  the salt or ion pair is never docked; scores are empirical docking scores in kcal mol⁻¹ (mean ± SD over seeds), never
  affinities.
- **Molecular dynamics.** What the text may say of the simulations is fixed: the outcome by the rules set before the
  runs, always with its limits (the two failed tests of the force-field terms at the sulfonamide nitrogen, the changes
  under backbone restraints, three runs per start of 100 ns with one force field); no rate, no barrier, no free
  energy. 11_Molecular_dynamics/README.md has the same statements with their numbers.
- **Structure.** 1 Introduction, 2 Computational methods, 3 Results (3.1–3.8), 4 Discussion (4.1–4.3, limitations in
  4.3), 5 Conclusions; then the back matter in the Elsevier order: CRediT authorship contribution statement, Declaration of competing interest, Funding, Data availability, Appendix A. Supplementary material, Declaration of generative AI and AI-assisted technologies in the manuscript preparation process, References. The Word file of
  the manuscript and the PDF of the supplementary material are the documents for submission; the PDF of the
  manuscript is a preview (`pdf-max-figure-height` keeps a tall figure and its caption on one PDF page without
  changing the Word files).

## Front matter and statements

The author details are part of the sources: the `author-block` field in the YAML header of `main/manuscript.md` and
`si/manuscript.md` holds the authors with their affiliation letters, the affiliations, the corresponding author with
the e-mail address, the e-mail addresses and the ORCID iDs. The `keywords` list of `main/manuscript.md` prints after
the abstract. The funding statement, the CRediT roles, the declaration of competing interest and the declaration on
the use of generative AI are sections of the back matter of `main/manuscript.md`. The highlights and the graphical
abstract are files of their own.
