Start X is the crystal pose, N the docked near-native pose and R the docked ring-rotated pose. The rows N1 to N3 are the runs from start N; in the column headings N2 is the sulfonamide nitrogen of the ligand. C, R and I are the shares of crystal-like, rotated-like and intermediate frames of the last 20 ns, which give the verdict. The time of the rule is the first frame of the first visit of at least 1 ns in the crystal-like state, counted from the release of the ligand, with the equilibration stage in which it lies; the next column gives, for the runs from start R, the first frame with the configuration of the crystal pose at N2 and the first frame that is not rotated-like (the runs from X and N keep the configuration of the crystal pose and have no rotated-like frame). Ligand RMSD: heavy atoms against the crystal pose of 6RL9, mean ± SD over the production and mean of its last half. Hydrogen bond N2–H···OG1(Thr199): share of the production frames. Cα RMSD from 6RL9: mean over the production. ^a^ Sensitivity run with zinc set 2s, which failed its validation run in one part (@tbl:s-md-gates). ^b^ Sensitivity run with candidate terms at N2 that were not accepted (@tbl:s-md-gates). ^c^ The verdict is MIXED, so this is no conversion but the start of the first crystal-like visit of at least 1 ns. All times of the table lie in the equilibration, before the production: the ligand is released at the start of stage 4 and the production starts 1,300 ps later. In stage 4 the protein backbone is held with 250 kJ mol⁻¹ nm⁻², in stage 5 with 50, in stage 6 nothing is held; in all five runs from start R the first frame with the configuration of the crystal pose at N2 and the first frame that is not rotated-like lie in stage 4. The production of the nine runs with set 2e holds 0 rotated-like frames of 90,000. The shares are not populations, and the times are single events with frames 10 ps apart: no rate, barrier or free energy follows from them. That the crystal-like state is kept and not left again belongs to the terms at N2 as built, which hold the nitrogen too stiffly; the two sensitivity runs enter no count.
