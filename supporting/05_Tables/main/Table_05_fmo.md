*I* = *E*(*N*−1) − *E*(*N*), *A* = *E*(*N*) − *E*(*N*+1), *η* = (*I* − *A*)/2, *χ* = (*I* + *A*)/2, *ω* = *χ*²/(2*η*). Orbital-based (Koopmans-type) values are compared in Table S16. The gas-phase *A* of sulfanilamide is negative (−0.099 eV): the extra electron is unbound and that ΔSCF anion is a basis-set artefact. The gas-phase cation is the anilinium tautomer, which is not the gas-phase minimum of the isolated cation [@uhlemann2021preferred].
