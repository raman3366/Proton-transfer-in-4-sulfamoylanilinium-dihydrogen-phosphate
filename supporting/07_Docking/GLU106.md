# Glu106 protonation in the two hCA II receptors: sensitivity test

5–6 October 2026. Every number below comes from a file written in this test; the file is named beside it.
Times are IST. Nothing that existed before was edited: the receptors, the stored runs, `VALIDATION.md`,
the manuscript and the facts are as they were.

New files: `dock/glu_protonation_variant.py`, `dock/run_glu106_sensitivity.sh`, this file, and the two
variant directories `dock/T3_6RL9_glu106GLU/` and `dock/T4_3HS4_glu106GLU/`.

## 1. The finding that was tested

Both prepared hCA II receptors carry Glu106 as the neutral acid: `prep_report.json` of `T3_6RL9` and of
`T4_3HS4` says "PROPKA pKa 8.91 > 7.4: protonated", and `receptor.pdbqt` has HE2 on OE2 with the
charges of Meeko's GLH template. PROPKA was run on the protein without the zinc. In the enzyme Glu106
is expected to be a carboxylate that accepts the hydrogen bond of Thr199 OG1–H, while Thr199 OG1
accepts the N–H of the zinc-bound sulfonamide. The paper does not mention the protonation of Glu106.

## 2. What was changed

`dock/glu_protonation_variant.py --target <T> --resnum 106` edits the prepared receptor instead of
preparing a new one (a new preparation is not bit-reproducible and would change many atoms at once):

| | 6RL9 (`T3_6RL9_glu106GLU`) | 3HS4 (`T4_3HS4_glu106GLU`) |
|:--|:--|:--|
| removed | HE2 of Glu106 (charge +0.296, type HD) | the same |
| recharged (type unchanged) | CB +0.047 → +0.041, CG +0.125 → +0.055, CD +0.303 → +0.041, OE1 −0.252 → −0.548, OE2 −0.479 → −0.549 (OE1, OE2 stay OA; C atoms stay C) | the same |
| charge of the residue | 0.000 → −1.000 | 0.000 → −1.000 |
| pattern taken from | the 12 other Glu of the same file, which all carry one pattern | the 12 other Glu of the same file |
| other lines of `receptor.pdbqt` | 2504 of 2510, byte-identical and in order | 2492 of 2498 |
| `receptor.pdb` | the HE2 line removed, 4073 other lines byte-identical | 4056 other lines |
| sha256 `receptor.pdbqt`, reported → variant | `f335c736…` → `0ad2c59c…` | `5e9ade19…` → `ee2972fd…` |

Source: `variant.json` in each variant directory (full hashes there). The reported hashes are those of
`prep_report.json`, so the edited receptors are the docked ones. The charges are not invented: Meeko
0.8.0 types and charges the polymer from residue templates (GLU, GLH), so every deprotonated mid-chain
Glu of a receptor has the same charges, and the script copies the charge and type columns of those
residues as text; it refuses if they disagree. The serial numbers of the other atoms are kept, so one
number is unused. The heavy atoms were not moved; the two C–O bonds of the new carboxylate therefore
keep the lengths of the minimised acid (1.210 and 1.327 Å in 6RL9, 1.207 and 1.329 Å in 3HS4).

in both prepared receptors, so the hydrogen-bond direction Thr199 → Glu106 that the enzyme needs is in
place and Thr199 OG1 is free to accept from the ligand. The extra proton sat on the other oxygen, OE2,
which also accepts the backbone N–H of Arg246. Removing it leaves a carboxylate with two hydrogen
bonds and changes no other hydrogen.

## 3. What was run

**Settings.** Read from the stored `results.json` of the reported runs and used unchanged:
exhaustiveness 32, seeds 1–10 (1–20 for the two production AutoDock4Zn jobs of sulfanilamide), the 20 Å
box of `box.json`, 20 modes within 3 kcal mol⁻¹, ETKDG conformers for the validation runs and for
acetazolamide, the DFT geometries for the production runs of sulfanilamide, Vina 1.2.7 (`AutoDock Vina
f458505-mod`). The docking drivers `run_vina.py` and `run_ad4zn.py` were used as they are. One setting
differs: `--cpu 2` instead of `--cpu 3`, because the machine is shared; the next paragraph shows that it
does not change a result. The ligand input of every seed of every variant run is byte-identical to the
input of the stored run it is compared with (`comparison.json` →
`ligand_inputs_identical_all_seeds`, true for every run).

**Baseline reproduced.** Three stored runs of the unmodified 6RL9 receptor were repeated at `--cpu 2` in
a copy of the drivers outside the repository: sulfanilamide with Vina, with Vinardo and with AutoDock4Zn
(stored maps), 10 seeds each. All 30 `out.pdbqt` files are byte-identical to the stored ones (every pose
and every score), as are the 30 ligand inputs (`T3_6RL9_glu106GLU/baseline_check.json`,
`baseline_check_ad4zn.json`). The stored runs are therefore the baseline, and they were not repeated
further.

**AutoGrid.** The stored maps were made on the x86 VM on 27 September 2026, the date in the log (`dock/T*/ad4zn/receptor_tz.glg`
and `maps_report.json`): `autogrid4` reporting "AutoGrid 4.2.7.x", `$Revision: 1.160 $`, OpenMP, 32
threads, parameter file `AD4Zn.dat`, grid parameter file `receptor_tz.gpf`. That program was built here
for aarch64 from the official source, in a new prefix, with the system compilers (g++ 13.3.0, autoconf
2.71, automake 1.16.5); no existing environment was touched and micromamba was not needed.

- Source: `https://github.com/ccsb-scripps/AutoGrid`, tag `v4.2.9` (commit `6d2847be…`), archive
  sha256 `d48eb3c72eb3708016ee27c297e2b7c3ad1e3d10e3ea2b0ea0cba95d4dcfbaf7`. Its `main.cpp` is
  `$Revision: 1.160 $` and prints "4.2.7.x" unless the build defines a package version; the autotools
  build defines 4.2.8, so the new log says "AutoGrid 4.2.8" for the same source revision.
- Build: `/root/cqc-sfa-tools/autogrid/build.sh` (the record of the build): `autoreconf -i`,
  `../configure`, `make`, as the README says. Two departures. The header `default_parameters.h` was
  generated with `sh` instead of `csh`, which this machine lacks; the generator consists of `echo`,
  `egrep` and `sed` commands only. The installed binary was compiled with `-O2 -ffp-contract=off`, so
  that aarch64 does not fuse multiply–add where x86-64 cannot; a second binary with the default flags
  is kept beside it.
- Binary: `/root/cqc-sfa-tools/autogrid/bin/autogrid4`, sha256 `298da61f…` (in
  `autogrid_validation.json`).

**Runs.** Sulfanilamide (neutral and sulfonamidate) and acetazolamide (neutral and anion), in both
variants, with the run names of the stored runs:

- Vina and Vinardo, 21 runs of 10 seeds: every validation and production run of these four species that
  the paper reports for hCA II. A production job of acetazolamide repeats its validation run bit for bit
  in the stored data (`baseline_prod_twin_identical`), so it was docked once and stands for both.
- AutoDock4Zn: 8 runs: sulfanilamide in both variants from ETKDG conformers (validation, 10 seeds) and from the DFT geometry (production, 20 seeds), the sulfonamidate in 6RL9 (validation), acetazolamide in both variants and the acetazolamide anion in 3HS4 (validation; the production jobs of acetazolamide repeat them bit for bit in the stored data). The six input-geometry diagnostics were not run (section 8).

## 4. Baseline against variant

"Glu106 acid" rows are the stored runs of the paper, analysed again here; "Glu106 carboxylate" rows
are the new runs. Both were analysed by the validation code itself (`analyse.analyse_job`:
symmetry-corrected heavy-atom RMSD in the receptor frame, the site checks Zn–N ≤ 2.3 Å and Thr199
OG1···N ≤ 3.3 Å, PoseBusters), with the gate formulae of `analyse.main`. RMSD and score are mean ± SD
over the seeds; "best of seeds" is the lowest top-pose RMSD; "pass" beside a distance is the fraction
of seeds whose top pose meets the cutoff. The gate columns are given for the runs that have a gate in
the paper (validation runs); PoseBusters was run here on the variant runs that have a gate and taken
from the stored validation analysis (`dock/analysis/summary.json`) for the baseline, whose RMSD and
score means this analysis reproduces for all 16 such runs (`agrees_with_stored_analysis`). The physics
pass fraction is 1.0 in every one of those runs, before and after, with one exception named in section
6. Source: `comparison.json` in each variant directory; the tables are printed by
`glu_protonation_variant.py --tables`.

## 5. What the changed receptor does to a fixed pose

Two rescoring checks without a search (`vina --score_only`) separate the scoring from the search. They
are not numbers of the reported protocol.

**Vina and Vinardo.** The top pose of every seed of eight stored sulfanilamide runs (80 poses) scores
the same in the reported receptor and in the variant: change 0.000 kcal mol⁻¹ for every pose

## 7. Conclusion

Deprotonating Glu106 changes no Vina or Vinardo result beyond search noise (a fixed pose scores
identically; no verdict changes), but it does change AutoDock4Zn numbers that the paper reports: neutral
ligands score 0.04–0.07 kcal mol⁻¹ better, the sulfonamidate 0.37 kcal mol⁻¹ worse, and the
ring-rotated orientation of sulfanilamide gains 0.013–0.018 kcal mol⁻¹ on the near-native one, which is
of the size of the separation the paper reports (0.008–0.039). The success fractions follow: in 6RL9 the DFT geometry no
longer gives the crystal pose in all 20 seeds but in 15 (0.61 ± 0.04 → 1.34 ± 1.30 Å), the validation
run goes from 5 to 3 of 10, the 3HS4 cross-dock from 10 to 9 of 10 (0.62 ± 0.03 → 0.84 ± 0.72 Å, the
strict gate still passed), and the sulfonamidate in 6RL9 from 3 to 7 of 10 (1.12 ± 0.32 → 0.86 ± 0.33 Å),
which now meets the strict gate. The zinc and Thr199 contacts, the presence of the near-native pose in
every seed, the score order of acetazolamide and sulfanilamide and the paper's reading that AutoDock4Zn
cannot separate the two ring orientations are unchanged; the test adds that which orientation ranks
first depends on the protonation of a residue 4.7–4.8 Å from the ligand as well as on the input
geometry.
