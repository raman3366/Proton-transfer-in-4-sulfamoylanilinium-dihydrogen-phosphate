## 2. The model class [`md/qm/n2_common.py`: `FEATURES`, `LADDER`, `features`, `fit_model`, `write_frcmod`]

### 2.1 Form

E(model) = E(rest) + Σ p_j f_j(geometry) + constant. E(rest) is the force field as built without the terms that may
change: the three angles at N2, every torsion about N2–S, every torsion about Zn–N2 [`points.json`,
`force_field.terms_taken_out_for_rest`: the three angles and the two H–N2–S–C4 terms; all others are zero as built].
Every f_j is a function of the geometry alone, so the energy is linear in every p_j and a fit is a least-squares
problem with linear and a few quadratic bounds. Checked: with the parameters as built, rest + Σ p f equals the total
for all 36 old points to 7 × 10⁻⁷ kcal mol⁻¹ [`points.json`, `checks`]; with test numbers in all 17 parameters, the
topology that tleap builds from the frcmod equals rest + Σ p f to 0.0008 over 228 geometries [`leap_test.json`,
`model_class_against_topology`].

### 2.2 The terms

Atom types as MCPB.py made them: M1 zinc; Y1, Y2, Y3 the nitrogens of His94 (NE2), His96 (NE2), His119 (ND1); Y4 the
ligand N2; sy sulfur; hn the hydrogen on N2; ca C4; o the sulfonyl oxygens [`ff/inputs/CA2_mcpbpy.frcmod`].

| Group | Internal coordinate, atoms | AMBER form | Free parameters, as fitted | frcmod line | Bound of the plan |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| angle | Zn–N2–S; Zn–N2–H; S–N2–H | K (θ − θ₀)² | per angle K and B = −2Kθ₀ (functions θ², θ): **6** | `M1-Y4-sy K θ₀`, `M1-Y4-hn …`, `hn-Y4-sy …` | K 10 to 300; θ₀ within 15° of the quantum-chemical optimum |
| tor_H | H–N2–S–C4 (HN21–N2–S–C4) | Σ PKₙ [1 + cos(nφ − γₙ)], n = 1, 2, 3 | cₙ on cos nφ (PK = \|cₙ\|, γ = 0 for cₙ > 0, 180° for cₙ < 0): **3** | `ca-sy-Y4-hn 1 PK γ −1` / `−2` / `3` | \|cₙ\| ≤ 5 |

| tor_ZnN_S | N(His)–Zn–N2–S, the three histidines with the SAME term | PK [1 + cos(3φ − γ)] each | a on Σ cos 3φ and b on Σ sin 3φ (PK = √(a² + b²), γ = atan2(b, a)): **2** | `Y1-M1-Y4-sy 1 PK γ 3`, the same for `Y2`, `Y3` | PK ≤ 5 |
| improper | N2 against the plane of Zn, S, H; angle ω = Zn–HN21–N2–S, the order tleap writes | PK [1 + cos(2ω − γ)] | c on 1 − cos 2ω (γ = 180° for c > 0: favours a planar nitrogen; 0 for c < 0): **1** | `M1-hn-Y4-sy PK γ 2` | \|c\| ≤ 5 (taken as for a torsion; the plan names no number) |
| tor_ZnN_H | N(His)–Zn–N2–H, the three histidines with the same term | as tor_ZnN_S | **2** | `Y1-M1-Y4-hn 1 PK γ 3`, `Y2`, `Y3` | PK ≤ 5 |

Windows for θ₀, from the optimised small model (all atoms free, B3LYP/6-31G*, task `qm_opt`; Zn–N2–S 107.74°,
Zn–N2–H 122.76°, S–N2–H 109.08°; pinned in [`qm_optimum.json`] with the checksum of the geometry file): 92.74 to
122.74°, 107.76 to 137.76°, 94.08 to 124.08°. The terms as built have 117.55°, 107.41° and 118.06°: the value for
Zn–N2–H, taken from the construction of the hydrogen, lies 0.35° outside its window.

**Why these and not more** (choices inside what the plan allows):

- *Cosines only about N2–S.* The group C4–S(O₂) has a local mirror plane through N2–S–C4, so the intrinsic term is
  even in the torsion. The oxygens sit near ±120° from C4, so series on H–N2–S–O and Zn–N2–S–O would repeat the
  series on C4 (exactly for n = 3, with the opposite sign for n = 1 and 2); they stay zero.
- *About Zn–N2 only the threefold term, the same for the three histidines, with a free phase.* With equal
  coefficients the onefold and twofold terms of three nitrogens 120° apart cancel; different coefficients per
  histidine would be 6 to 18 more parameters for a direction that 4 old points sample. The phase is free (a cosine
  and a sine part) because nothing makes this term even.
- *The improper is even in the pyramid.* 1 − cos 2ω has the same value for the two configurations at N2. **A term
  odd in ω, which would favour one configuration over the other directly, is not in the class**: it would be a bias
  on the very question of the simulation, and it is not a standard improper.
- *No term on the N(His)–Zn–N2 angles, on Zn–N2, on charges or Lennard-Jones*: outside point 2 of the plan.

### 2.3 How tleap takes the terms [`md/qm/n2_leaptest.py` → `leap_test.json`; a throwaway build]

The small model was built twice with the inputs of gate G-R (kept in `ff/inputs/`, checksums in `ff/SHA256SUMS`): as
built, and with a test frcmod loaded last that holds one term of every class (arbitrary numbers).

- **The torsions through the zinc exist in the topology as built, with zero coefficients** (periodicity 3): the
  three N(His)–Zn–N2–S, the three N(His)–Zn–N2–H, Zn–N2–S–C4, Zn–N2–S–O1 and –O2.
- **An explicit dihedral type with a non-zero coefficient replaces the zero term on exactly these atoms**: the test
  values arrived on the four-atom sets named in the table and nowhere else; every other bond, angle, torsion,
  improper, charge and Lennard-Jones type is identical [`differences_outside_the_allowed_classes`: none]. A series
  of three terms and a phase of 90° are accepted. The frcmod is written with IDIVF = 1, so PK is the coefficient.
  Every periodicity 1 to 3 of a refitted torsion is written, zero included, so that no term of an earlier file
  (MCPB.py's zero term with periodicity 3, the two terms as built) survives beside the new ones.
- **1–4 scaling.** A proper torsion brings its end atoms into the list of 1–4 pairs, scaled by 1/1.2 (charges) and
  1/2.0 (Lennard-Jones). These pairs exist already, because the zero torsions exist: 151 pairs in both builds,
  identical with their scaling; the 335 exceptions of OpenMM's non-bonded force are identical. The zinc has C4, O1
  and O2 among its 1–4 partners in both. **A non-zero coefficient changes no non-bonded interaction.**
- **The improper** is created by tleap when the type line exists: one term on Zn, HN21, N2 (central), S, periodicity
  2; there is none at N2 as built. It adds no 1–4 pair.
- The topology rebuilt from the kept inputs equals the kept topology of gate G-R term by term
  [`rebuilt_as_built_equals_kept_topology`].

Not yet shown: the same in the full protein system through `md/build_topology.py --n2-frcmod`. The types M1 and Y1 to
Y4 occur only at the site, so the same atoms are meant; the builder's own check of the terms at N2 was written for
three angles and one series and has to be run on a file with more terms before the systems are rebuilt.

### 2.4 The ladder, the fit and the control of over-fitting

| Model | Groups | Free parameters | Fitting points needed (8 per parameter) |
|---|---|---|---|
| L1 | angle, tor_H | 9 | 72 |
| L2 | L1 + tor_Zn | 12 | 96 |
| L3 | L2 + tor_ZnN_S | 14 | 112 |
| L4 | L3 + improper | 15 | 120 |
| L5 | L4 + tor_ZnN_H | 17 | 136 |

One constant is fitted beside (it is no force-field parameter). The fitting set has 36 + 108 = 144 points with the
main list and 180 with the reserve.

- **Fit** (`n2_common.fit_model`): least squares of E_QM − E(rest) with equal weights, as a bounded problem: the
  bounds of section 2.2 are constraints of the fit (for θ₀ they are linear in the centred form K(θ − r)² + b(θ − r),
  r the optimum angle: |b| ≤ 2K·15°), solved with SLSQP from K = 50, θ₀ = r, all coefficients zero. A fitted model
  is therefore inside the bounds by construction; a parameter resting on a bound is reported. Checked on the old
  points: where no bound is active the bounded solution equals the solution of the normal equations (L2 to L4 of
  section 1).
- **Ridge**: a penalty ridge × N × Σ c² on the Fourier and improper coefficients only (towards zero, the value
  MCPB.py gives the terms through the zinc), not on the angles; ridge ∈ {0, 0.01, 0.1}.
- **Leave-one-out** (`n2_common.leave_one_out`): every fitting point predicted by the same bounded fit to all the
  others; its root mean square is the number of condition 5.
- **Choice, fixed here.** Candidates: the five models times the three ridge values, each eligible only with at least
  8 fitting points per parameter and a converged solver. Taken: the smallest leave-one-out r.m.s.; among candidates
  within 5 % of it, the one with the fewest parameters, then the larger ridge. It must pass `n2_gate.py preview`; if
  it does not, the next in the same order is tried. The first that passes goes to the final test, once. Everything
  in this paragraph uses the fitting set only.
- **The program** is `md/qm/n2_fit.py`, written now: it loops over these 15 candidates with the two functions above,
  reads energies only through `n2_common.load_points('fit')`, and writes `fit/candidates.json` and one
  `fit/MODEL_<rank>_<name>.json` per eligible candidate, in the order of preference, for the gate. The arithmetic is
  in `n2_common.py` and is the same code the gate uses for condition 5.

### 3.3 The split, and how the code keeps it [`split.json`, `split.json.sha256`]

The list in its order is cut into blocks of four; in every block one point is drawn for the test set by
`numpy.random.default_rng(20261006)`. Written with the checksums of all 192 geometry files; the checksum of
`split.json` itself is `fc9cea71f53f41c49aa94be55877b5331df09e1cce8471474dfa8dec1cbb4693`. The 36 old points are
fitting data; R and N as built are not used (docked geometries of the neutral molecule, not rigid points).

- **One door to the energies**: `n2_common.load_points(purpose)`. With `'fit'` it skips every point flagged "test"
  before its result file is opened and asserts that none came through. With `'gate'` it stops unless the
  environment has `N2_FINAL_TEST=1`, and it appends time, process and command line to `test_set_access.log`.
- `n2_gate.py final` refuses to run when `gate/n2_gate.json` exists. `n2_gate.py preview` uses `'fit'` only.
- `split.json` is read only through `split_record()`, which stops when the file no longer has its checksum.
  `n2_points.py` refuses to write a different split once a result of a new point exists.
- Each result file carries the set of its point, and the loader stops on a contradiction with the split.
- **The log of the batch holds no energy** (one line per point: identifier, set, exit code, seconds), and
  `n2_batch.py status` prints counts only, so that following the batch shows no test energy.
- What the code cannot prevent: someone opening `qm/<id>.json` of a test point by hand. The rule is that nobody
  does before the final test.

### 3.4 Prefix rule

The batch computes in the order of the list and starts no new point after its deadline. The data of the gate are
the points finished then; none is left out afterwards. A point whose SCF does not converge in two attempts is
reported as failed and is not replaced. The final test needs at least 20 test points (condition 1).

## 6. The fit

### 6.1 What is fitted, and how the points of part A are treated [`md/qm/n2_fit.py`, `md/qm/n2_common.py`]

- **Data**: the 36 rigid points of gate G-R and the new points flagged "fit" whose single points had ended when the
  batch stopped starting points (prefix rule). The program reads energies only through `n2_common.load_points('fit')`;
  it opened no result file of a test point, and none was in the repository (section 5.3).
- **Quantity**: y = E_QM − E(rest), with E_QM = B3LYP/6-31G* + D3(BJ) and E(rest) the force field without the terms
  that may change; equal weights; one constant for all points.
- **The 25 points of part A are fitted like every other point.** They are part of the data the plan names, and
  leaving them out would take the inversion path and the hydrogen moves out of the fit. The number of part A is
  therefore in-sample; the same number from the leave-one-out errors of these points is given beside it and is the
  honest estimate of what condition 3 measures. Nothing is weighted towards part A.
- **Candidates and their order** as fixed beforehand (sections 2.4 and 5.1, point 2): five models times three
  ridge values; exact leave-one-out by refitting; ranking number = the larger of the leave-one-out r.m.s. and the
  leave-one-out r.m.s. against one reference; eligible with at least 8 fitting points per parameter.
- **Standard errors** by linear theory in the parameters of the fit (K, b, coefficients), carried to θ₀ and to
  amplitude and phase by the delta method; a parameter resting on a bound has none. Checked on synthetic data
  (section 5.5).
- **What the program says about the gate** is an expectation, computed from the linear model on fitting data
  (conditions 3, 4, 5 and the pre-test); `md/qm/n2_gate.py` was not run on real data in this stage, in either mode.

### 9.7 What this test cannot show

- **The small model in vacuum.** 64 atoms without Thr199, the pocket wall and water: the gate compares two
  descriptions of the same atoms; it gives neither the barrier of the inversion in the protein nor which
  configuration the protein prefers (`md/GR.md`, section 1).
- **A rigid ligand at unrelaxed placements.** Every point of the gate holds the ligand body at the crystal geometry
  and the site fixed; 153 of 192 new points are places the rigid protein would not allow (the builder's flag,
  section 5.2), and 13 fitting points have
  hydrogen contacts shorter than any the rehearsal runs visited. A force field with fixed Lennard-Jones walls
  cannot follow quantum chemistry there whatever its terms at N2, so part of the misfit says nothing about the
  nitrogen. The two relaxed paths of section 9.4 are the only test at relaxed geometries, and they are two paths.
- **One level of theory, no counterpoise estimate.** B3LYP/6-31G* with the two-body D3(BJ) term; the basis-set
  superposition error, which acts against the dispersion term (worth −9.09 kcal mol⁻¹ between X and Rx
  [`d3_check.json`]), is not quantified; the three-body term is left out (+0.30 there by `md/GR.md`, section 6.1).
  The optimum of condition 6 is without a dispersion term.
- **Condition 3 in-sample, conditions 1 and 2 unread.** How either candidate does on unseen points is known only
  through the leave-one-out error.
- **A refused model says nothing against the terms as built beyond what gate G-R said**: gate G-R stays failed
  (part A 3.08), and that failure is unrepaired.
